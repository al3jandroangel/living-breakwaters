# Santa Monica Breakwater — Circulation & Tracer Transport
**Phase-resolved modeling of infragravity-driven circulation and passive-tracer transport around a remnant high-energy breakwater.**

Companion repository for the ICCE 2026 poster *"Living Breakwaters in High-Energy Waves: Phase-Resolved Circulation & Tracer Transport around the Santa Monica Breakwater"* (Angel, Gomez & Gallien, UCLA).
